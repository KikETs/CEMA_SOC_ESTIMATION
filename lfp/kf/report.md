## Main KF interpretation

- The representative hysteresis baseline is `hysteresis_2rc_ekf`; oracle-initial-SOC mean MAE is `13.4762%`.
- Plain 2RC-EKF is an ablation, not the representative KF result. Hysteresis did not improve the aggregate result in this implementation; that negative result is retained.
- UKF remained numerically finite but produced much larger SOC error. It is reported rather than removed.
- Plateau MAE is substantially worse than edge-region MAE, consistent with weak voltage observability on the flat LFP OCV plateau.

## v2.1 — revised-search rerun

### D0 — declared rationale

v2's SOC-MAE objective admitted a degenerate optimum: q_soc→0 makes the SOC channel open-loop
(≈ oracle-init Coulomb counting) while Vp/H states absorb the voltage residuals. A filter exists
to recover from unknown initial SOC, so v2.1 encodes initial-error recovery into selection.
If no trial recovers, that is itself the finding: no Q/R setting reconciles recovery with
plateau accuracy on this data.

- Branch: **feasible_off_boundary**.
- Optuna infeasible fraction in the decisive representative-fold cycle: `20.500%`.

### D4 — NMC overlay

- Scope: one pre-declared representative NMC fold, `DST`, at 0, 25, and 45 °C. The held-out DST records were used only for this final trade-off evaluation.
- Fixed parameters: the existing NMC fold×temperature training-only 2RC-EKF fits and selected `q_vp`/`r_voltage` values. The NMC baseline has no hysteresis state, so `q_h=0` is explicitly reported rather than inventing an H process.
- Sweep: eight log-spaced `q_soc` values from `1e-12` to `1e-4`, plus exact open-loop Coulomb-counting SOC. The evaluation mask, steady interval (`elapsed >=1800 s`), and recovery criterion (`|SOC error|<1.5 percentage points` within 1800 s) match the v2.1 definitions.
- Exact open-loop produced a slice-unweighted oracle steady MAE of `0.1613%`, but neither +5 pp nor −5 pp recovered in any of the 3 temperature records.
- Among finite points, the lowest slice-unweighted oracle steady MAE was `1.0709%` at `q_soc=1.9307e-10`; all +5 pp and −5 pp runs recovered, with median first-hit times of `27.27 s` and `20.19 s`, respectively.
- This NMC exhibit reproduces the same accuracy/recovery conflict: the best oracle steady accuracy is obtained by exact open-loop, which cannot correct initial SOC error. `nmc_tradeoff_audit.json` records the six training files, three held-out files, hashes, selected temperature-specific noise settings, and zero train/test intersection.

### Honesty and limitations

- All completed and infeasible Optuna trials are retained in SQLite and CSV under `results_v2_1/optuna/`.
- v1, v2, and their numerical artifacts were hash-checked as immutable.
- The NMC source was available on a separate local worker and is pinned by file hashes in `nmc_tradeoff_audit.json`; the remote LFP repository does not contain or refit the NMC source data.
- NMC and LFP rows share an identical overlay schema, but the NMC baseline has no H state and only three temperatures in the selected fold. Therefore `q_h=0` is an explicit not-applicable value, not a fitted zero.
